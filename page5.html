To You 🤎

Maybe we won't stay the same forever. Maybe life will pull us into different directions. Maybe one day these conversations, these late-night talks, these small moments, will become memories. But if that day ever comes, I hope whenever you remember me, you remember peace. Because you were one of the softest, most beautiful parts of my life. You made ordinary days feel lighter. You made silence feel comforting. You made me care in ways I never expected myself to. And honestly, a version of me will always belong to you. No matter what changes, I will always be grateful that I met you.

Things I'll Always Remember

your sleepy texts
your random anger
your pretty eyes
your smell
your childishness
your comfort
your laugh
the way you cared

Thank You For Existing 🤎

— Rishikesh
